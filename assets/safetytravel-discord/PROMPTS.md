# Built-in ImageGen prompt set

All images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Files under `assets/branding`, `assets/mascot`, `assets/ui-screens`, and `assets/illustrations` were used only as visual references for identity, palette, and illustration style.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Discord application icon, square 1:1, designed for circular avatar crop
Input images: Image 1 is the exact SafetyTravel elephant mascot identity reference; preserve the same face, mint skin, large teal inner ears, dark navy oval eyes, pink cheeks, forehead tuft, trunk shape, orange travel vest and soft vector rendering
Primary request: a close-up portrait of the same mascot from the upper chest upward, looking directly at the viewer and smiling warmly with an open happy mouth; head and both ears fully visible and dominant
Scene/backdrop: clean soft radial mint-to-pale-aqua background filling the entire square, with a subtle lighter circular halo behind the head
Style/medium: polished soft vector-like mascot illustration matching Image 1 exactly, rounded shapes, gentle gradients, clean edges, friendly travel-safety brand
Composition/framing: face centered; symmetrical, head occupies about 70 percent of the canvas; both ears and forehead tuft inside a generous circular safe area; a small portion of orange vest visible at the bottom; readable at 32 pixels
Lighting/mood: bright, welcoming, trustworthy, cheerful
Color palette: mint, turquoise, teal, deep navy, white, pink cheeks, restrained orange vest accent
Constraints: no hands; no waving pose; no full body; no backpack visible; no map pin badge; no text; no letters; no numbers; no logos; no watermark; no transparent or dark corners; no crop through ears or tuft; original mascot identity must remain recognizable
```
